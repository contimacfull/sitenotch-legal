Support

Sitenotch · Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Version 1.0

About

Sitenotch is a mobile app for documenting field work: jobs, issues with photos, receipts, notes, PDF reports, simple invoices and backups.

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue on the project’s GitHub page. Issues are public, so do not post personal data, client details, photos of documents, invoices or backup files.

https://github.com/contimacfull/sitenotch-legal/issues

Or write by e-mail for technical support, bug reports and help using the app: oadsupport@gmail.com. Do not attach client documents, invoices or backup files. Developer: Alexandru Dan, an individual developer (not a company).

Common questions

  • Where is my data? Only on your device. There is no account and no server.
  • How do I back up? Profile → “Create backup” (entries and PDFs) or “Export all data” (including photos). Keep the file somewhere safe.
  • How do I delete my data? Profile → “Erase everything”, or uninstall the app. Files you shared or exported are not deleted by the app.
  • Dictation does not work in my language. Dictation runs only with on-device recognition. Install the language pack in your phone’s speech settings or type the note.
  • Receipt text is wrong. Text recognition gives suggestions; always check the fields before saving.
  • Is Sitenotch free? Sitenotch is free to download. The free version has limits: up to 3 projects, 3 receipts and 3 reports. Lifetime Premium is a single one-time purchase that removes these limits and adds voice notes, drawing on photos, client acknowledgement on reports, invoices and automatic reading of receipts. It is not a subscription and there is no recurring fee.
  • How do I buy or restore Premium? Profile → “Sitenotch Premium”. Tap the buy button, or “Restore purchases” if you already bought it with the same Apple Account (or Google account). The purchase is handled by the App Store (or Google Play); the app never sees your payment details.
  • How do I get a refund? Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play under their own rules. If a store refunds the purchase, Premium turns off and everything you saved stays on your device.
  • How do I recover my data from a backup? Profile → “Restore from a file”, then choose your backup file. If you protected it with a password you need that password; it cannot be recovered.
  • What happens if I delete the app? The data stored by the app on the phone is deleted with it and the publisher cannot recover it, so make a backup first. Your Premium purchase belongs to your store account and can be restored.
