Privacy Policy

Sitenotch · Last updated: November 4, 2026 · Version 1.0

Availability: Sitenotch is released first for iPhone (iOS) through the Apple App Store. The Android version on Google Play follows shortly afterwards, subject to Google Play approval. Until a version appears in a store, it is not available there; where this page mentions Apple or Google, the text for the other store applies once that version is released.

1. In short

  • Sitenotch has no account, no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own, and no server of the publisher.
  • Your jobs, photos, receipts, notes, reports, invoices, client lists and business details stay on your device.
  • The app does not send your content to the publisher or to third parties. Your content leaves your device only when you choose to share or export it.

2. Who is responsible

The publisher is Alexandru Dan, an individual. Because the app does not transmit your data to the publisher, the publisher does not receive or hold the content you enter in the app.

3. What the app stores on your device

  • Profile: name, company, role, language, theme, currency, avatar.
  • Jobs, issues with before/during/after photos, notes, receipts (and photos of receipts), reports and invoices (PDF files and records), client lists, your business details (including tax numbers, bank details, logo and stamp images you add), backup timestamps, and which tips were shown.
  • Photos are re-encoded when added, which removes camera and location metadata from the stored copy. The original in your photo library is not changed.
  • The app keeps a safety copy of its data file on the device.
  • Drawings you add on top of a photo (lines, arrows, shapes, highlights and short text labels you type). They are saved as data next to the photo; the photo file itself is not changed.
  • If you use the optional client signature on a report: the signer’s typed name, a drawing of their signature, the date and time from your device’s clock, the statement shown (“I acknowledge receipt of this report.”, in the language of the app) and SHA-256 checksums of the report files. They are kept in the app’s data and printed on a confirmation page of a separate signed copy of the report. The app does not identify or verify the signer and asks for no other information about them. Please obtain the person’s agreement before collecting their name and signature.
  • SHA-256 checksums of report PDF files, saved with each report so the app can tell you whether the file on the phone is the same as the one that was saved.
  • Unsaved edits of a problem (text, photos and drawings you have not saved yet). They are kept in the app’s data until you save or discard them, so that they are not lost if you leave the screen or close the app. They are part of the app’s data and therefore of backups and exports.
  • Premium status: after the store confirms a purchase, the app keeps a copy of the result in the device’s secure storage (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore) so that it also works offline. This copy is not part of the app’s own backup files, and restoring a backup cannot switch Premium on or off.
  • If you turn on the optional app lock: whether the lock is on. The app asks the device to confirm your identity (Face ID, fingerprint or device passcode) when it opens and only receives whether the check succeeded; it never receives biometric data.
  • If you protect a backup with a password: the app does not store the password or the key made from it.

4. What leaves your device

  • Nothing is sent by the app to the publisher. The app is not designed to connect to the internet, and the Android version does not request the Internet permission, so Android does not allow the app to open network connections.
  • When you share a PDF or a backup file, the system share sheet hands it to the app or service you choose (mail, messaging, cloud storage and so on). That destination’s own privacy policy applies; the publisher does not receive it.
  • The only web address the app can open is a link to the publisher’s legal pages in your browser, when you tap it.
  • Premium purchase: Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). You enter payment details only in Apple’s or Google’s own screens; the app and the publisher never receive them. To know whether Premium is active, the app asks the store service of the operating system, which may contact Apple or Google; those requests are made by the operating system, not by code of the publisher, and Apple’s or Google’s policies apply.

5. Permissions and how they are used

  • Camera: to photograph issues, finished work and receipts, and, only if you turn it on and open the AR screen, to show the optional AR mascot (iOS). The camera starts only when you use these features.
  • Photo library: through the system picker you choose photos or a logo/stamp to add. The app can access only the items you select.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: only while you dictate a note or an invoice. Recognition is requested on the device. If your language is not available for on-device recognition, the app does not use dictation and does not fall back to a server.
  • The app does not ask for your location, contacts, calendar or notifications.
  • Face ID or device authentication: only for the optional app lock, if you turn it on.
  • On Android, dictation is offered only on phones that support on-device recognition (Android 13 or later).

6. Third-party components and services

  • Receipt text recognition runs on your device: Apple Vision on iOS, and Google ML Kit with a model bundled in the app on Android. Google states that ML Kit processes images and text on the device and does not send them, or the recognised text, to Google servers. Google also states that ML Kit may send diagnostic and usage metrics to Google (such as device model and OS version, the app’s package name and version, per-installation identifiers, performance measurements, API settings and error codes) and may contact Google for fixes and model updates; Google’s privacy policy applies to any such metrics. The Android app does not request the Internet permission, so the app itself cannot open network connections; we have not verified ML Kit’s behaviour on every device, and Google Play services on your phone may act separately. The iOS app uses no Google SDK.
  • Dictation uses the speech recognition built into iOS or Android in on-device mode.
  • The app includes open-source software under its own licences; see the open-source notices page.
  • Apple and Google provide the operating system and the stores; their own policies apply to your use of them.
  • Purchases use Apple StoreKit on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android, which exchange information with the stores through the operating system’s store services.

7. Backups and device transfer

  • Backup files and full exports are created only when you ask for them. When you create one, the app asks whether to protect it with a password. With a password, the file is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your password using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256); the app does not store the password, and if you forget it nobody can open the file. Without a password the file is not encrypted; keep it somewhere safe.
  • Android: the app is configured so that Android’s cloud backup and device-to-device transfer do not include its data. Use the app’s own backup and export instead.
  • iOS: the app does not change iOS’s defaults, so files in the app’s storage can be part of the device or iCloud backups that you control in your device settings.
  • A regular backup (“Create backup”) contains your entries and data (including photo drawings, signers’ names and signature drawings), your report and invoice PDFs and signed copies, and your logo and stamp, but not your photos. A full export (“Export all data”) also contains the photos. Neither contains your Premium status.

8. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. “Erase everything” in the Profile screen removes everything the app keeps: jobs, photos and the drawings on them, receipts, notes, unsaved edits, reports and signed PDFs (with the names and signatures in them), invoices and the safety copy. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the device. Files you exported or shared are outside the app and are not deleted by it.

Deleting a signed report in the app removes the signed PDF and the original PDF from the device, after a separate confirmation. “Erase everything” also removes signature data and photo drawings. Copies you already shared or exported are outside the app.

9. Security

Your data is protected by your device’s own protections (screen lock, system encryption). Keep your device and your backup files safe. Backups you protect with a password are encrypted by the app; backups made without a password are not. The app can check on your device whether the phone appears to be rooted or jailbroken and shows a note; the result is not sent anywhere, and such a check cannot guarantee security.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children.

11. Your rights

Because the publisher does not collect your app data, there is no copy of it for the publisher to access, correct or delete; you control and can delete it yourself in the app. If you contact the publisher, the information you send is used only to answer you. You may also lodge a complaint with your data-protection authority.
